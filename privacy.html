Privacy & Transparency

Your privacy matters at Wazir Research Lab.

Welcome to Wazir Research Lab. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, stored, used and handled when you visit this website or voluntarily communicate through its available contact methods.

Website Privacy Notice

Wazir Research Lab does not ask visitors to submit unnecessary personal information. Information is primarily collected when you choose to contact the lab through the website or another provided communication channel.

01

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the website, information may be provided voluntarily through the contact form, email or external professional platforms.

Information provided directly by you may include your name, email address and the content of your message, together with any other information you choose to include in your inquiry.

Basic technical information may also be processed by hosting, security or third-party services used to operate the website. The exact information collected by those services depends on their own systems and policies.

02

Website Messages & Supabase

Messages submitted through the website contact form are sent to the connected database service, Supabase, so that inquiries can be received and managed through the website's protected administration system.

The contact form is currently designed to submit the following message information:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your message
  • The date and time associated with the submitted record

Visitors should not submit passwords, API keys, payment credentials or other highly sensitive information through the contact form.

03

How Information Is Used

Information voluntarily provided to Wazir Research Lab may be used for legitimate communication and service-related purposes.

  • Responding to inquiries and messages
  • Discussing research, data, web or digital projects
  • Understanding project requirements
  • Providing requested information or services
  • Managing website communications

Information is not intentionally collected simply for unrelated purposes.

04

Cookies & Similar Technologies

The website itself may use browser technologies required for basic functionality. In addition, external services used by the website may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies according to their own systems.

These technologies may support functionality, security, analytics, advertising or user experience features where such services are enabled.

05

Third-Party Services & External Links

Wazir Research Lab may link to or use third-party services including Supabase, Fiverr, LinkedIn, GitHub Pages and other external platforms.

When you leave this website or interact with an external service, that service may collect or process information under its own privacy policy, terms and security practices.

Wazir Research Lab does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites and services. Review their policies before submitting information directly to those services.

06

Data Security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect website information and restrict administrative access.

However, no Internet transmission, website, database or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should therefore avoid submitting confidential credentials or information that is not necessary for your inquiry.

07

Children's Privacy

This website is not specifically directed toward children, and Wazir Research Lab does not knowingly request personal information from children.

08

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website functionality, services, data practices or other relevant circumstances change.

The Last updated date shown on this page may be changed when a material revision is published.

09

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or the website's information practices can be sent through the Contact page or by email at zuhaibwazir558@gmail.com .

You can also visit the About page to learn more about Wazir Research Lab.
